## 🤖 How to Prompt AI Image Generators

### ChatGPT (DALL-E 3) — Best Practices
- **Be explicit about style first:** Start with `"Photorealistic photograph,"` or `"Editorial-style photo,"` before describing the scene.
- **Specify ethnicity and context:** Say `"Black Kenyan women"` to avoid defaulting to Western representations.
- **Describe lighting:** `"Soft natural window light"` or `"golden hour outdoor light"` — DALL-E responds well to lighting cues.
- **Add technical camera feel:** `"Shot on Sony A7IV, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field"` — this signals quality.
- **Include brand colours as props/clothing:** `"wearing a lime green headwrap"` or `"lime green accent wall in background"`.
- **Avoid prohibited content:** Never reference real people by name or real medical photos.
- **Aspect ratio:** Add `"--ar 16:9"` or `"in landscape orientation"` for website banners; `"portrait orientation"` or `"1:1 square"` for social.

### Gemini Imagen — Best Practices
- **Describe the mood sentence first:** Start with an evocative sentence like `"A moment of quiet dignity and collective joy..."` then get specific.
- **Request a specific number of people:** `"two women"`, `"a group of five"` — Gemini handles this better with explicit numbers.
- **Style anchor:** Add `"--style photorealistic"` or reference a visual style: `"editorial NGO photography style, similar to UNICEF campaign imagery"`.
- **Colour anchoring:** Reference the colours in the *environment*, not just clothing: `"lime green foliage, bright Nairobi sunlight"`.
- **Negative prompts (if available):** Add `"no stock-photo feel, no western setting, no sterile hospital environment"`.
