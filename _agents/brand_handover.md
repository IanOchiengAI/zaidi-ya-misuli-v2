## 🛠️ Visual System Decisions
For future assistants, these are the established "rules" for the Zaidi Ya Misuli visual identity:

1. **The Logo Mark:** 
   - *Description:* A vibrant red rose blooming from a green stem with two leaves shaped like flexed muscular biceps, branching out into green roots at the base.
   - *Icon-Only Rule:* For wearable merchandise (Wristbands, T-shirts, Tote Bags), use **only the icon** without the "ZAIDI YA MISULI" wordmark to maintain a premium streetwear aesthetic.

2. **The Color Palette:**
   - **Lime Green (#32CD32):** High energy, health, life.
   - **Rich Black (#111111):** Grounding, authority.
   - **Pure White (#FFFFFF):** Clarity, accessibility.

3. **Photography Style:**
   - *Ethnicity:* Always specify "Black Kenyan women/men" to ensure cultural authenticity.
   - *Framing:* Dignified, thriving, and joyful. Avoid any "pity-based" framing of disability.
   - *Technical:* Photorealistic editorial style, 85mm lens feel, golden-hour or bright window natural light.

4. **Merchandise Highlights:**
   - **A-Frame Banners:** Specifically used the lightweight horizontal "bean" pop-up shapes for accessibility and ease of set-up.
   - **Wristbands:** Four options provided ranging from matte silicone to executive fabric/metal finishes.
   - **♿ Wheelchair-Specific Merch:** Includes prompts for branded spoke guards (wheel billboards), side-hang gear bags, and reflective safety decals.
